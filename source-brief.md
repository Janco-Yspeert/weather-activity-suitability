## Senior / Lead Engineer Take-Home Exercise

This is more open-ended than a real ticket would be, and that's deliberate. In day-to-
day work the shape of a task gets worked out together as a team, and you'd help
define it. We've left this one loose so we can see that thinking.
What we're most interested in is how you work through problem: the decisions you
make, the assumptions you commit to, and how you get there. Where you'd normally
check an open question with a product manager, note the question and the
assumption you went with.
We use AI daily and ask that you do the same. Model choice doesn't matter and free
tiers are fine.
The exercise
Build a backend service that takes a city or town and ranks how good the next 7
days will be for each of these activities:

- Skiing
- Surfing
- Outdoor sightseeing
- Indoor sightseeing

Open-Meteo provides the weather data. Persist it rather than calling the API on
every request. How you model, store, and refresh it is part of the problem.
This is a backend exercise, so please don't build a front end.
We're not looking for volume. A focused submission that reasons well beats an
exhaustive one.
Stack
Node.js and GraphQL. Storage is your call.
What to submit
A public GitHub repo. Two things matter, in this order:

1. How you worked. However your thinking actually happened, whether that's
   upfront planning, an evolving AI session, notes, commits, or any mix, show it
   to us. We want to follow your reasoning and see the calls you made, not read
   a write-up. Don't polish it. If you cut something or ran out of time, a line on
   why is more useful than the thing itself.
2. The service, plus a short README: what you built, how to run it, and your
   assumptions.
   Let us know once you've finished.
